### 📐 Chalk Line

Chalk Line is a sports field analysis tool that lets you draw and overlay measurement lines directly onto game footage and images. It's designed to help coaches and analysts quickly assess player positioning, spacing, and movement patterns with precision.

**Key Features:**

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| ✏️ Draw & Annotate | Draw and annotate lines on sports images and video frames |
| 📏 Measure | Measure distances and angles on the field |
| 🔗 Share | Quickly share annotated footage with players and coaching staff |
| 🏟️ Multi-sport | Supports a wide range of sports and field layouts |
